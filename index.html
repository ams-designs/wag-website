WHO WE ARE

A home for the
power of words.

The Writers' Association of the Gambia stands for the people who write, imagine, document, and share the experiences of our nation.

From poetry and fiction to essays, oral traditions, and contemporary storytelling, Gambian literature carries the richness of our culture and the possibilities of our future.

WAG's website is a place to discover our literary heritage, learn about writers, find opportunities, and connect with a community that believes every voice deserves the chance to be heard.
